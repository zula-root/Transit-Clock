About this clock

A clock made of timetables.

Each minute it shows a real train, ferry or bus that is timetabled to leave at exactly that minute, somewhere in the world. There is one for all 1,440 minutes of the day.

The time on screen is always the time on your own clock, read off a station clock somewhere else. The chevrons at the top choose which part of the world that is.

The idea is borrowed from the literary clock, which tells the time with sentences from novels. Timetables turn out to be full of sentences too.

Times come from the operators’ published timetables. Left open on a stand, the page keeps the screen awake.
